## Categories
account_access: login, passwords, account locks, OTP or authentication
billing: payments, refunds, invoices, subscriptions or charges
technical_issue: errors, crashes, unavailable services or incorrect behaviour
feature_request: requests for new capabilities or improvements
## Priorities
low: suggestion, cosmetic issue or non-urgent request
medium: normal work is affected, but a workaround exists
high: important work is blocked or several users are affected
critical: production outage, security incident or nearly all users affected

The priority must represent business impact—not emotional words such as “urgent.”
